## Priority Issues

### [P0] The sidebar never shows where you are
**What.** `components/nav-main.tsx:53,71` render `SidebarMenuButton` / `SidebarMenuSubButton` with no `isActive`; neither `nav-main.tsx` nor `app-sidebar.tsx` imports `usePathname`. The primitives already accept and style `isActive` (`components/ui/sidebar.tsx:500,519,672,687`) — it was simply never passed. Compounding it, `app-sidebar.tsx:41` hardcodes `isActive: true` on **Tablero only**, so `defaultOpen` (`nav-main.tsx:48`) leaves all eight other groups collapsed on every route, *including the one you are standing in*.

**Why it matters.** A GERENTE is 71 links deep in a nine-section tree, navigating dozens of times per shift, interrupted by service. Land on `/dashboard/inventory/waste` and the Inventario group is closed and nothing is highlighted — you must expand a 22-row group and re-read it just to confirm you're where you meant to be. This one defect drives both H1 (1/4) and H6 (1/4).

**Fix.** In `nav-main.tsx`: `const pathname = usePathname()`; pass `isActive={pathname === subItem.url}` at `:71`, `isActive={pathname.startsWith(item.url)}` at `:53`, and replace `defaultOpen={item.isActive}` at `:48` with `defaultOpen={item.items?.some(i => i.url && pathname.startsWith(i.url))}`. Delete the now-dead `isActive: true` at `app-sidebar.tsx:41`. **Roughly 8 lines.**

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

Twelve pages ship their own branch selector anyway, and six do **not** read the global scope: `analytics/page.tsx:339`, `audit/page.tsx:547`, `reports/page.tsx:259`, `reports/schedule`, `company/communications`, `equipment/compliance`. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] The accessibility floor is below usable, and it's concentrated in shared chrome
**What.**

- **Filter labels not associated with controls**: `evidence:236,249,270,289,298` and `audit:498,511,534,546,558,566` use bare `<label className="text-sm font-medium">` with no `htmlFor`. Six consecutive unnamed comboboxes on the audit page. (`civil-protection:386–427` does it correctly — the knowledge exists in the codebase.)

**Why it matters.** PRODUCT.md sets no formal WCAG target, and that's a legitimate call — but "general best practices for keyboard navigation and screen reader support" is the stated bar, and silent async results plus an untrapped modal are below it. **Fix.** Skip link in the dashboard layout; a single `aria-live="polite"` region wired to the toast system; port `execute`'s modal to the existing `Dialog` primitive; add `htmlFor`/`id` to the 11 filter labels; convert the 4 `<div onClick>` to `<button>`; translate the `sr-only` strings.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P2] 305 raw palette classes and 39 shadow overrides route around a token layer that is already correct
**What.** 305 `(bg|text|border)-(blue|green|red|amber|…)-[0-9]{2,3}` across 52 dashboard files, plus 39 `shadow-*` overrides across 13. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

## Questions to Consider

1. **What is the one number the owner opens this to see?** `app/dashboard/page.tsx` renders eight sections and answers eight questions at equal weight — which is the same as answering none. `inventory/page.tsx` knows its answer ("el gerente abre aquí para empezar a operar"). Why doesn't the home route?
2. **If the header branch control is "the single source of scope" — why do twelve pages ship their own, and why do six of them win?** Either AD-1 is real and six pages are bugs, or it isn't and the header control is decorative. Both are defensible; shipping both is not.
3. **DESIGN.md bans the hero-metric template. `evidence:173` is five hero metrics counting an in-memory array; `execute:253` is three whose values are the words "Ver Tareas."** Is the rule wrong, or is nobody reading it? An unenforced design system costs more than no design system.
4. **The `expenses` approval gate is the best UX in this product. Why does it exist exactly once?** Whatever produced it — a spec, a good day, one engineer who cared — is the thing worth institutionalizing as a `<ConfirmDestructive>` primitive, not as a pattern doc.
5. **What is the actual role of `/dashboard/executive` versus `/dashboard`?** Both claim to be the owner's overview. If `executive` is the real single pane of glass, it should *be* `/dashboard` for ADMIN, and today's home should be the GERENTE's. Role-based routing at the root eliminates an entire class of "which dashboard?" hesitation.
6. **A Mexican compliance product strips the accents off "Protección Civil."** DESIGN.md says brand lives in precise details. If the diacritics on the NOM-002 page aren't the precise detail, what is?
