#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The dashboard is deeply rooted in Mexican HORECA operations (NOM-251 sanitary compliance, multi-branch inventory shrinkage, shift staffing, and group exception queues). The "Consejo del Grupo" pattern is a high-conviction decision tailored for multi-unit directors. However, the surface suffers from an architectural accumulation problem: rather than making decisive choices about what an operator needs in the first 30 seconds, it stacks every dashboard widget ever built into a monolithic vertical scroll. "Cumplimiento NOM-251" is presented in four separate places (AreaCard, TabbedMetrics, ExecutiveSummary, and ComplianceRadialChart), diluting focus and creating ambiguity over which metric is canonical.

#### Overall Impression
Pulso's dashboard possesses genuine domain depth and robust fault tolerance (via Suspense and SectionErrorBoundary), but it currently operates as a "kitchen sink" accumulator. It forces the user to navigate 7 vertical sections and 5 separate Recharts visualizers where 2 focused panels would deliver faster operational clarity.

#### What's Working
- **Resilient Fault Isolation**: Every major dashboard section is decoupled behind its own `Suspense` and `SectionErrorBoundary`, ensuring a slow analytics query never halts the operational exception feed.
- **Purpose-Built HORECA Domain Model**: Widgets like "El Consejo del Grupo" and NOM-251 compliance tooltips reflect authentic restaurant chain operations rather than generic SaaS templates.
- **Persistent Tenant & Branch Scope Context**: The top header (`BranchScopeControl` + dynamic breadcrumbs) cleanly clarifies whether the operator is looking at the entire chain or an individual branch.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Keyboard Navigation Hazards & Unintended State Loss**: In `components/dashboard/dashboard-tabbed-metrics.tsx` (lines 131–186), a global `window` keydown listener catches `Escape` to wipe the `pulso_selected_branch` cookie and redirect to `/dashboard`, while `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight` cycles tabs from anywhere on the page.
  - **Why it matters**: A user pressing `Escape` to dismiss a popover or search dropdown unintentionally wipes their branch selection and triggers a full page navigation. Arrow key navigation inside forms or components gets hijacked.
  - **Fix**: Remove the global cookie-wiping `Escape` listener and restrict arrow navigation strictly to focused tab triggers.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

- **[P1] Information Redundancy & High Cognitive Clutter**: The page stacks 7 vertical sections with 5 charts. "Cumplimiento NOM-251" is displayed in 4 distinct widgets, and open incidents appear in 4 places with slightly differing labels and presentations.
  - **Why it matters**: Overloaded operators cannot quickly identify the most critical issue. Cognitive load fails 5 of 8 checklist criteria (Single Focus, Chunking, Visual Hierarchy, One Thing at a Time, Minimal Choices).
  - **Fix**: Unify compliance into a single canonical command bar, eliminate redundant radial and pie charts from the home view, and reorganize detailed analytics into focused sub-tabs.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`

- **[P1] Design System Floor & Palette Drift**: 16 instances of sub-12px type (`text-[10px]`, `text-[11px]`), hardcoded emerald colors (`emerald-500`, `#10b981`), off-ramp radii (8px), and bottom accent stripes (`border-b-2`) contradict `DESIGN.md`.
  - **Why it matters**: 10px and 11px text is unreadable on kitchen tablets mounted at arm's length. Arbitrary colors weaken the visual authority of the OKLCH design tokens.
  - **Fix**: Enforce the 12px Label Floor across all components, migrate emerald utilities to `bg-success/10 text-success`, and align radii to 6px/10px.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable audit`

- **[P2] Fragmented Scope Adaptation**: The page layout does not meaningfully adapt between "All Branches" and a single selected branch. When looking at a single branch, executive comparison widgets ("Mejor Sucursal" / "Requiere Atención") still render, while Area Cards continue to show "Grupo" badges on local exceptions.
  - **Why it matters**: Single-unit managers are distracted by irrelevant chain comparisons, while chain executives must scroll past operational widgets to see cross-branch performance.
  - **Fix**: Conditionally render the Executive Branch Ranking when `alcanceEsTodas` is active, and switch to a streamlined Shift & Task Execution view when scoped to a single branch.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`
