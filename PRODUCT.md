# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Restaurant groups with 3 to 15 branches across Mexico. The primary audience is business owners and ADMINs who oversee the entire operation — they need a single pane of glass across locations for compliance, inventory, labor, and operations. Secondary users are operations managers (GERENTES) running daily workflows at the branch level, supervisors executing checklists, and employees confirming tasks via WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Pulso is the all-in-one operations and compliance platform for Mexican HORECA chains. It replaces scattered spreadsheets, paper checklists, and last-minute audit scrambles with a unified system for workflows, NOM-251/NOM-035 compliance, inventory, labor management, and WhatsApp-driven task execution. Success means restaurant groups run cleaner operations, pass regulatory audits without stress, and reduce waste and labor costs — all from one place.

## Positioning

The only platform that combines operations management, NOM compliance, inventory, and HR for Mexican HORECA chains — accessible from the web and from WhatsApp, so nothing falls through the cracks.

## Brand Personality

Confident, sharp, operational. Pulso speaks with the authority of someone who knows the HORECA industry inside out — no fluff, no jargon, just the right tool for the job. The tone is direct and professional, but never cold. It's the system a busy restaurant owner trusts to keep their branches running and compliant without adding overhead.

## Anti-references

- **Not bureaucratic or government-like.** Even though Pulso handles NOM compliance, the interface should never feel like a government system. No heavy borders, no dense tables with tiny type, no institutional gray. Compliance is a feature, not the identity.
- **Not generic SaaS.** This is purpose-built for Mexican HORECA chains, not a rebranded project management tool. The experience should feel native to the industry — familiar to someone who runs restaurants, not someone who runs Jira.
- **Not cold or impersonal.** Operational doesn't mean robotic. The brand should feel like a capable partner, not a faceless platform.

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence without bureaucracy.** The interface earns trust through clarity and speed, not through institutional-looking forms or dense tables. Every screen should make the next decision obvious.

2. **Operational at every pixel.** Nothing decorative. Every element serves a real operational purpose — reducing friction for a manager who has 15 branches to oversee and no time to hunt for features.

3. **Compliance as a byproduct, not a chore.** NOM-251/035 compliance should emerge naturally from good daily operations, not feel like extra work. The system makes the right thing the easy thing.

4. **One platform, one truth.** Inventory, labor, workflows, and compliance share the same data model. A manager should never enter the same information twice or wonder which screen has the real numbers.

5. **Works where the team works.** WhatsApp is not an add-on — it's a first-class interface for employees who never sit at a desk. The web dashboard is the command center; WhatsApp is the field terminal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target. Follow general best practices for contrast, keyboard navigation, and screen reader support. The primary audience (restaurant owners and managers) uses the dashboard on desktop and tablet; employees interact primarily via WhatsApp.
